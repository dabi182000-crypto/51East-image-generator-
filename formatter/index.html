    How it works

    Each photo runs through the same three steps. Everything happens on a canvas in this tab — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    1. Whiten. A flood fill starts at the frame edge and snaps every connected near-white pixel to pure #FFFFFF. If more than 93% of the frame floods, the product itself is white and merged with the background, so the fill is abandoned rather than erasing the product.
    2. Fit. The result is scaled proportionally and centred on an exact square white canvas — never cropped, never stretched.
    3. Encode. Quality steps down through a ladder only until the file meets its size target, so fine detail survives instead of being compressed away to hit a byte count.

    Built as a companion to the AI product-photography generator — that one writes new photographs from your references and needs an OpenAI key on a server. This tool needs nothing.
